# SERVICES PAGE REQUIREMENT SCRIPT

## PAGE OBJECTIVE

Design a premium, modern Services page for my personal portfolio.

This is **not** an agency website or a software company website.

It should feel like a highly skilled software engineer helping businesses across Punjab adopt modern digital technologies, AI, automation, and scalable web solutions.

The page should communicate trust, craftsmanship, transparency, and long-term support.

Avoid corporate buzzwords.

Avoid pretending to be a large team.

The tone should feel personal, confident, and solution-oriented.

Every section should answer one simple question:

**"How can I help your business grow?"**

The overall design should feel modern, premium, interactive, and visually engaging with smooth animations, modern illustrations, diagrams, hover effects, and subtle transitions.
